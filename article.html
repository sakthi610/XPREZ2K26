The 75th Year Platinum Jubilee Celebrations (1952—2027) of Alagappa Chettiar Government College of Engineering and Technology (ACGCET), Karaikudi commenced with great enthusiasm and pride on 20th July 2026 at Murugappa Hall, marking the beginning of a year-long celebration honoring the institution's remarkable journey of academic excellence, innovation, and service to society.

Opening Ceremonies

The inaugural function began with the Reception and Registration, followed by the garlanding of the statue of Dr. Alagappa Chettiar, paying tribute to the visionary founder whose ideals continue to inspire generations. The celebrations gained further significance with the foundation stone laying ceremony for the Alagappa Incubation Foundation and Research Park (AIFRP) by the Chief Guest, symbolizing the institution's commitment to fostering research, innovation, entrepreneurship, and industry collaboration.

The Formal Session

The formal session commenced with Tamil Thai Vazhthu, followed by an invocation through a graceful classical dance performance, invoking divine blessings for the success of the Platinum Jubilee celebrations. A specially curated documentary showcasing the rich history, milestones, achievements, and legacy of ACGCET was presented, highlighting the institution's outstanding contributions over the past seven and a half decades.

The dignitaries were then welcomed onto the stage, followed by the traditional Lighting of the Kuthu Vilakku, signifying the pursuit of knowledge and enlightenment. The gathering was warmly welcomed by the Principal, who reflected on the institution's glorious past and its vision for the future.

Platinum Jubilee Logo Launch

One of the major highlights of the ceremony was the launch of the official Platinum Jubilee logo, representing seventy-five years of excellence and the institution's aspirations for the years ahead. The logo launch was accompanied by the felicitation of the Chief Guest:

"Research, innovation, interdisciplinary collaboration, and technological advancement are the pillars that will shape future engineers — and ACGCET stands as a beacon of all four."

— Dr. K. Ramesha, M.Sc., Ph.D.
Director, CSIR—Central Electro Chemical Research Institute (CECRI), Karaikudi

AIFRP Roadmap Presentation

The event also featured felicitations by the Secretary of the Platinum Jubilee Committee, followed by the presentation of the AIFRP Roadmap by the Strategic Committee, outlining ambitious initiatives aimed at strengthening research infrastructure, entrepreneurship, industry partnerships, and academic excellence.

The Committee Convenors presented an overview of the various activities planned throughout the Platinum Jubilee year, including:

  • Academic conferences and seminars
  • Alumni engagement programs and reunions
  • Student competitions and technical events
  • Cultural events and arts showcases
  • Community outreach and social responsibility programs
  • Commemorative initiatives and publications

These activities are designed to celebrate the institution's enduring legacy while building bridges between the past, present, and future of ACGCET.

Cultural Program

Adding vibrant energy to the celebrations, the students presented a lively group dance performance, showcasing the creativity, enthusiasm, and cultural spirit of the ACGCET community — a fitting tribute to the institution's tradition of nurturing holistic development alongside academic rigor.

Conclusion of the Inaugural

The inaugural ceremony concluded with the Vote of Thanks delivered by the Vice Principal, expressing gratitude to the distinguished guests, alumni, faculty members, staff, students, organizing committees, and everyone who contributed to the successful commencement of the Platinum Jubilee celebrations. The gathering concluded with the National Anthem, followed by a group photograph session involving the Platinum Jubilee Committee and Sub-Committee members, capturing a memorable beginning to this historic milestone.

The inaugural function beautifully reflected the values and vision of Alagappa Chettiar Government College of Engineering and Technology — celebrating 75 years of excellence in engineering education while reaffirming its commitment to innovation, research, leadership, and nation-building. The Platinum Jubilee celebrations stand as a tribute to the institution's glorious past and a promising step toward an even brighter future.
