A Proud Milestone: ACGCET Celebrates Graduation Day 2026

A Historic Beginning to a Landmark Celebration

The Graduation Day Ceremony of Alagappa Chettiar Government College of Engineering and Technology (ACGCET), Karaikudi, was celebrated with immense pride and grandeur, marking a significant milestone in the lives of the graduating Class of 2025. The ceremony brought together distinguished dignitaries, faculty members, proud parents, alumni, and graduates to honor the academic achievements and celebrate the beginning of a new professional journey.

The event commenced with a warm welcome from the Principal, Dr. A. Sivanantharaja, who highlighted the rich heritage of the institution founded by the visionary philanthropist Dr. Alagappa Chettiar. He reflected on the college's remarkable growth since its establishment and emphasized its commitment to academic excellence, research, innovation, and holistic student development.

A Year of Outstanding Achievements

During his annual report, the Principal proudly presented the institution's achievements for the academic year 2025—2026. He highlighted the college's continued autonomous status, NBA and NAAC accreditations, recognition in the NIRF 151—300 band, achievements of the Institution's Innovation Council, establishment of startup incubation facilities, increasing internships, research activities, and the significant contribution of alumni towards institutional development. He also announced the grand celebrations of the 75th Platinum Jubilee, with ambitious infrastructure development plans to strengthen industry—institution collaboration.

The report further showcased the outstanding accomplishments of every department, including impressive placement records, research publications, patents, student innovations, Smart India Hackathon participation, GATE achievements, sports excellence, entrepreneurship initiatives, international internships, and industry collaborations. The Electronics and Communication Engineering Department, in particular, celebrated its NBA accreditation and received a state-of-the-art Flying Probe Tester worth â‚¹1 crore, generously donated by alumnus and Chief Guest Er. S. R. Sabapathi through CSR initiatives, significantly strengthening the department's testing and research capabilities.

Inspiring the Next Generation

The ceremony's highlight was the inspiring Graduation Day Address delivered by the Chief Guest, Er. S. R. Sabapathi, Founder, Chairman, and Managing Director of QMAX Test Equipments Pvt. Ltd., and a distinguished alumnus of the Electronics and Communication Engineering Department. Sharing his remarkable journey from a humble background to building a globally recognized technology company, he encouraged graduates to embrace innovation, perseverance, discipline, curiosity, and continuous learning. He reminded students that failure is never the opposite of success but rather an essential step toward achieving it. Through practical examples from engineering and his own entrepreneurial experiences, he motivated graduates to think creatively, solve real-world problems, and always strive to contribute beyond their assigned responsibilities.

Conferment of Degrees

Following the addresses, the formal presentation of degree certificates commenced. Heads of various departments presented the undergraduate and postgraduate candidates before the President of the Graduation Ceremony for the conferment of degrees. Students from Civil Engineering, Mechanical Engineering, Electrical and Electronics Engineering, Electronics and Communication Engineering, Computer Science and Engineering, Information Technology, and various postgraduate programmes proudly received their degrees in the presence of their families and faculty members, marking the successful completion of years of dedicated academic pursuit.

The graduates then took the professional pledge, reaffirming their commitment to honesty, integrity, ethical practice, national service, and lifelong learning. The President of the ceremony officially conferred the degrees, encouraging every graduate to uphold the dignity of their profession and contribute positively to society.

The ceremony concluded with the National Anthem and heartfelt congratulations to the graduating batch. As the graduates stepped out wearing their academic robes with pride, the occasion symbolized not just the completion of their academic journey but the beginning of a future filled with opportunities, innovation, and service to society. The Graduation Day served as a memorable celebration of achievement, determination, and the enduring legacy of ACGCET in shaping responsible engineers and future leaders.
