Our Story

How XPREZ Began

XPREZ was founded with a simple but powerful vision — to create a publication that truly reflects the energy, creativity, and academic spirit of ACGCET. What started as a small initiative by a group of passionate students has grown into the college's most eagerly anticipated publication.

Over the years, XPREZ has evolved into a comprehensive newsletter covering everything from campus events and technical innovations to alumni interviews and creative writing. Each edition is a testament to the talent and dedication of the ACGCET community.

Today, XPREZ continues to grow — with a dedicated editorial team, an expanding archive of articles, and a readership that spans current students, alumni, and the broader engineering community.
