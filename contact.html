FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to the most common questions about XPREZ.

You can submit articles by using the contact form above and selecting "Article Submission" as the enquiry type. Include your article in the message body or mention that you'll attach it. Our editorial team will review and get back to you within 2—3 working days. In the future, we plan to launch a dedicated article submission system.
Any currently enrolled student or faculty member of ACGCET is welcome to contribute. We accept articles, photographs, poems, illustrations, event reports, and more. Alumni contributions are also welcome for the Alumni Voices section. We celebrate creativity from all departments and all years.
XPREZ recruits new team members at the start of each academic year. Announcements are posted on our social media channels and the college notice board. You can also express your interest by using the contact form and selecting "Join Editorial Team." We welcome writers, editors, designers, photographers, and social media enthusiasts.
XPREZ publishes an annual magazine edition that captures the entire academic year. In addition to the annual magazine, we publish articles, event reports, and campus news throughout the year on this website. Follow us on social media for the latest updates and publications.
Yes! All past magazine editions are available on the Magazine page. You can read them online or download PDFs of each edition. If you have trouble accessing any edition, please contact us using the form above.
